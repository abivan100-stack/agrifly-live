The problem → our answer

No real-time field intelligence

Farmers rely on slow, manual walk-through inspections.

Live sensor readings every few seconds.

Crop stress spotted late

Dry soil, heat or humidity changes are noticed after the damage starts.

Trends over time instead of one-off checks.

Wasted water, fertilizer, labour

Measured conditions to plan with, and less walking the field.

Hard to compare field zones

GPS-tagged readings, each with a place on the map.

How it works

Drone flies & sensors read

A 5-inch FPV drone carries BME280, BH1750, soil, gas and GPS sensors.

ESP32 sends over Wi-Fi

Every 2 s the ESP32 posts the readings and GPS position to the laptop server.

Live dashboard

Cards, charts and a map update instantly on any laptop or phone on the network.
