The problem, and our answer

No real-time field intelligence

Farmers rely on slow, manual walk-through inspections.

Live sensor readings every few seconds.

Crop stress spotted late

Dry soil, heat or humidity changes are noticed after the damage starts.

Trends over time instead of one-off checks.

Water, fertilizer and labour wasted

Resources are used without data on actual field conditions.

Measured conditions to plan with, and less walking the field.

Hard to compare parts of a field

Readings aren't tied to a location, so areas blur together.

Every reading is GPS-tagged and placed on the map.

How it works

  1. The drone flies and the sensors read

    A 5-inch FPV drone carries BME280, BH1750, soil, gas and GPS sensors.

  2. The ESP32 sends over Wi-Fi

    Every 2 seconds it posts the readings and its position to the laptop server.

  3. The dashboard updates live

    Cards, charts and a map update on any laptop or phone on the same network.
