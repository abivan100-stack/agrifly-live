AgriFly Open live dashboard

Smarter technology for smarter farming

AgriFly is a drone with a small sensor pack. As it flies over the field it sends readings every few seconds, tagged with its GPS position, to a live dashboard.

Testing now with an IR sensor. Temperature, humidity, light, soil and air sensors come next.
