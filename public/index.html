AgriFly is a drone with a small sensor pack. As it flies over the field it sends readings every few seconds, tagged with its GPS position, to a live dashboard. We are testing with an IR sensor now; temperature, humidity, light, soil and air sensors come next.

Why AgriFly

Four problems with how fields are checked today, and how AgriFly addresses each one.

What farmers deal with todayWhat AgriFly does
No real-time field intelligenceFarmers rely on slow, manual walk-through inspections. Live sensor readings every few seconds.
Crop stress spotted lateDry soil, heat or humidity changes are noticed after the damage starts. Trends over time instead of one-off checks.
Water, fertilizer and labour wastedResources are used without data on actual field conditions. Measured conditions to plan with, and less walking the field.
Hard to compare parts of a fieldReadings aren't tied to a location, so areas blur together. Every reading is GPS-tagged and placed on the map.

How it works

From the field to your screen in a few seconds.

  1. The drone flies and the sensors read

    A 5-inch FPV drone carries an ESP32 and its sensors. Testing now: an IR obstacle sensor.

  2. The ESP32 sends via Blynk or Wi-Fi

    Readings go to Blynk Cloud from any network, or straight to the laptop on the same Wi-Fi.

  3. The dashboard updates live

    Cards, charts and a map update on any laptop or phone on the same network.
